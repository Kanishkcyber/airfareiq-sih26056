Methodology

Statistical design proposed for SIH26056.
Index construction
AIₜ = 100 × Σ wᵣ(Fᵣ,ₜ / Fᵣ,₀)
Fᵣ,ₜ = representative fare for route r. Fᵣ,₀ = route base fare. wᵣ = traffic weight, with weights summing to 1.
Design principles
Traffic-weighted route basket
Median representative fares
T+45 → T+1 booking-window tracking
Transparent statistical core
ML reserved for prediction and anomaly layers
